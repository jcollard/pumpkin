# Asset licences

Every file in `assets/` is either **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication, no attribution required) or
**CC BY 3.0** (attribution required). There is nothing NC, editorial, personal-use-only or ripped from a game.

All models were downloaded from [Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza), which shows the licence per model. They were
then modified: rescaled to real-world size, re-centred, unused animation clips removed, some recoloured, and textures
resized and re-encoded. CC BY 3.0 allows modification as long as the change is indicated. Textures and HDRIs come
from Poly Haven and ambientCG (both CC0).

The vendored three.js addons in `vendor/three/addons/` are MIT, from three@0.187.0 (see `vendor/three/LICENSE`).
